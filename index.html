Research

Wavelet and machine-learning method for rapid quality analysis of pulmonary function tests

Co-author2024 to presentFirst Affiliated Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University

General practitioners need to know whether a spirometry test was performed well before they trust its numbers. We built a fast model that checks this automatically.

  • Combined wavelet analysis with an artificial neural network, using both the flow-volume curve and the numerical values from each spirometer report.
  • Classified reports against the ATS/ERS 2019 standards into one qualified class and eight anomaly classes.
  • Validated the model on a held-out test set so it can flag quality problems for clinicians automatically.
  • Co-wrote a 4,000-word article, submitted to Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing.

Autonomous rescue robot

Hardware engineer, designer and competition strategist · 2022 to 2024

A robot that explores a rescue maze on its own, recognises victims and delivers medical kits.

  • Computer vision with two vision-recognition modules and 360-degree panoramic cameras to read visual cues in the maze.
  • Breadth-first search at intersections to guarantee full maze coverage.
  • PID control for stable line tracking and obstacle navigation.
  • 3D-designed and printed kit-delivery system, touch-sensitive shields and a fishnet-style arm, kept light for speed.
3rd, World 2024RoboCup Junior World Championship, Super Team. Also 2nd at RoboCup Junior China 2024.

Faster aircraft boarding

Team leader, International Mathematical Modeling Challenge · 2022 to 2023

How should an airline board and unload passengers to save the most time?

  • Built a boarding and disembarking model and tested it on different boarding methods and passenger behaviours.
  • Analysed scenarios with standard deviation, z-scores, t-statistics, covariance and Chebyshev's inequality.
  • Used decision trees, Python and Pandas to simulate and visualise the results.
  • Modelled real-world friction such as carry-on bags and passengers who don't follow instructions.
2nd PrizeIMMC country round, 2022.
